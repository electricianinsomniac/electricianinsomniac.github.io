Tech / SaaS Product

SatSetSaji — QR Ordering & Kitchen Display for F&B SMEs

A QR-based ordering system for small and medium restaurants and cafés in Indonesia. Customers scan a QR code at their table, order from a digital menu, and pay online — while staff work from connected kitchen display (KDS), cashier, and reporting screens. Currently at the demo stage with a pilot project.

Deries Garment — Transforming a Family Production House into a Direct Brand Partner

Rooted in our family’s textile and garment journey in Tasikmalaya since 1999, Deries Garment specializes in comfortable, well-fitted telekung (prayerwear) and pouches. Joining as the next generation, I am leading the business transformation—shifting from traditional middleman distribution to an open, collaborative B2B manufacturing partner for prayerwear brands across Indonesia and Malaysia.
