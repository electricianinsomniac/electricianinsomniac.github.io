Rooted Since 1999. Transformed for Modern Prayerwear Brands.

The Family Foundation (1999)

Deries Garment began in 1999 as a family endeavor in Tasikmalaya, West Java—a region deeply known for its Muslim wear and embroidery heritage. Starting from a small-scale textile micro-business (UMKM), my parents steadily built our capabilities into a local sewing workshop (konveksi) and eventually into a dedicated garment production unit.

For over two decades, our focus was purely on making the product right. However, like many traditional manufacturers in Tasikmalaya, our distribution relied heavily on local wholesale collectors (pengepul) and closed referral channels—meaning many growing prayerwear brands could never access our production line directly.

My Role — Bridging Traditional Craftsmanship with Growing Businesses

Joining the family business with a background in engineering and digital problem-solving, my mission is to transform how Deries Garment connects with the market. Many prayerwear entrepreneurs and emerging brands struggle to find a dependable production house: they either face opaque middlemen, struggle to communicate custom pattern revisions, or have to juggle separate vendors for basic sewing, embroidery, and bags.

A Telekung Must Feel Right When You Pray, Not Just Look Good in Photos

Anyone who wears a telekung daily knows the frustration of an ill-fitting headpiece—fabric that slips at the forehead, pulls awkwardly at the chin, or requires constant readjustment during prayer. In our family, we treat the face opening (face-fit) as the heart of every telekung we make:

Tested in Real Prayer

Whenever we develop a new pattern, it isn't just measured on a table. Our family wears the sample for actual prayer, evaluating how it rests on the forehead, frames the face, and covers the chin. If it feels uncomfortable, we revise the pattern before moving forward.

Dedicated Face-Part Tailoring

Because the head and chin sections require specific stitching techniques, they are handled by experienced tailors who understand the tension and stretch needed for a comfortable fit.
